The Cultural Triangle — 8 Days

Multi-Day Tour

The Cultural Triangle

8 Days / 7 Nights 2–12 People 4.9 / 5.0

This eight-day journey traces the arc of Sri Lanka's greatest ancient civilisations — from the hydraulic genius of Anuradhapura to the sky-palace of Sigiriya, the cave frescoes of Dambulla, the royal city of Polonnaruwa and the sacred temples of Kandy. It is a journey through 3,000 years of living history, guided by scholars who breathe the stories of these stones.

Day-by-Day Itinerary

Day 1Arrival in Colombo — Colonial Heritage Walk

Your Heritage Travels representative meets you at Bandaranaike International Airport with a chilled welcome drink. Transfer to your colonial-era boutique hotel in Colombo Fort. After freshening up, an early-evening walk through the Pettah bazaar, Mount Lavinia beach, and the red-bricked façade of the Old Parliament building as the city turns golden. Welcome dinner at a heritage restaurant overlooking the Beira Lake.

3:00 PMAirport welcome & hotel check-in
5:30 PMColombo colonial heritage walk
7:30 PMWelcome dinner at Ministry of Crab (or equivalent)

Accommodation: The Kingsbury Colombo or equivalent 5-star heritage hotel.

Day 2Anuradhapura — The First Kingdom

Drive north into the Cultural Triangle. Anuradhapura was the first great capital of Sri Lanka — a city of soaring dagobas, ancient moonstone carvings and the oldest human-planted tree in the world: the Sri Maha Bodhi, grown from a cutting of the tree under which the Buddha found enlightenment, brought here in 288 BC. Walk the sacred precincts at dusk as the dagobas turn golden white against a violet sky.

7:00 AMDepart Colombo after breakfast
11:00 AMSri Maha Bodhi & Ruwanwelisaya dagoba
2:00 PMThuparamaya & Jetavanaramaya stupa
5:30 PMSunset at Tissa Wewa reservoir

Accommodation: Ulagalla Resort, Anuradhapura.

Day 3Sigiriya — Ascending the Lion Rock

An early start for the climb to Sigiriya at sunrise, before the heat and the crowds arrive. Your expert guide narrates the extraordinary story of King Kashyapa as you ascend past the legendary frescoes, through the Lion's Gate, to the summit plateau where the ruins of the royal palace command a 360° panorama of jungle and glittering reservoirs. Afternoon: Dambulla Cave Temple, a UNESCO site with five cave shrines and 150 statues of the Buddha.

6:00 AMSigiriya Rock climb at sunrise
9:30 AMWater gardens and museum
2:00 PMDambulla Cave Temple
5:00 PMVillage walk and local cooking class

Accommodation: Aliya Resort & Spa, Sigiriya.

Day 4Polonnaruwa — The Medieval Capital

Explore the medieval capital of Polonnaruwa by bicycle — the best way to absorb the scale and serenity of this royal city, which flourished from the 11th to 13th centuries. Highlights include the massive Gal Vihara rock carvings, the circular Vatadage, and the reclining Buddha that inspired generations of pilgrims. Afternoon wildlife drive in Minneriya National Park for the famous Elephant Gathering (seasonal, Jul–Oct).

Day 5Matale Spice Country — Journey to Kandy

Drive south through Matale's spice gardens — cinnamon, cardamom, cloves — where you can taste, smell and learn the stories of the spices that made Sri Lanka the most coveted island in the ancient world. Arrive in Kandy by afternoon. Evening: the Kandy Esala Perahera cultural show (or the permanent Kandyan dance performance), followed by a lakeside dinner.

Day 6Kandy — Temple, Market & Tea

Morning puja (offering) ceremony at the Temple of the Tooth Relic — a spiritual experience unlike any other. Visit the gem museum, the vibrant Kandy market, and in the afternoon, a private tour of a working tea factory in the hills above Kandy with a guided tasting of five grades of Ceylon tea.

Day 7Pinnawala & Return to Colombo

Visit the famous Pinnawala Elephant Orphanage to watch the river bathing — a joyful spectacle for all ages. Drive back to Colombo with a stop at a handloom silk weaving workshop. Farewell dinner at a rooftop restaurant with views over the Indian Ocean.

Day 8Departure — Until Next Time

A leisurely breakfast before your Heritage Travels representative transfers you to the airport in time for your flight. We hope Sri Lanka has earned a permanent place in your heart.
