Our Story

A Small Island with an Extraordinary Soul

Sri Lanka is not simply a destination — it is a feeling. The scent of cinnamon on a Colombo morning. The thunder of a monsoon over Sigiriya's emerald jungle. The stillness of a thousand-year-old dagoba at dawn. We founded Heritage Travels to share these moments — unhurried, authentic, and deeply human.

Every itinerary we craft is born from years of lived experience and genuine relationships with local communities, expert naturalists, and cultural guardians across the island. We are not a booking platform — we are your partners in discovery.
