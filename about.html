Who We Are

Born in Sri Lanka. Built for the World.

Heritage Travels was born in 2006 from a single conviction: that travel done well should change both the traveller and the place visited — for the better. Our founder, Nimal Perera, grew up in the shadow of Sigiriya, the son of a schoolteacher who spent weekends walking him through the ruins and stories of the ancient kings. That childhood shaped everything.

Today, our team of 24 passionate Sri Lankans — naturalists, historians, chefs, adventure guides and logistics specialists — crafts private journeys for international travellers who want more than photographs. They want to feel this island. To sit with a tea plucker at dawn. To hear a monk's chant echo through a 2,000-year-old stone chamber. To find a leopard watching them from a granite boulder at the hour before the sun sets.

We are not the largest tour company in Sri Lanka. We never want to be. We are the most devoted.

The People Behind Your Journey

Meet Our Team

Every one of us is Sri Lankan, every one of us is passionate, and every one of us will remember your name long after your trip is over.

Nimal Perera
Founder & CEO

Raised in the shadow of Sigiriya, Nimal has guided travellers through Sri Lanka's ancient landscape for over 20 years. He speaks four languages and knows a leopard's favourite rock in Yala by name.

Priya Rajapaksa
Head Naturalist Guide

A certified wildlife biologist and passionate birder, Priya leads our Yala and Sinharaja expeditions with infectious excitement. She has spotted over 380 bird species in Sri Lanka alone.

Ashan De Silva
Cultural Heritage Guide

A former archaeologist who worked on UNESCO restoration projects, Ashan brings the stones of Anuradhapura and Polonnaruwa to life with the authority of someone who has spent years reading them.

Dilini Fernando
Head of Travel Design

Dilini crafts each itinerary with the care of a novelist, weaving together places, timings, meals and moments into journeys that feel less like a schedule and more like a story.

What We Stand For

Our Values

🤝
Authenticity

We refuse to manufacture experiences. Every encounter — a village meal, a monk's conversation, a craftsman's workshop — is real, unscripted, and mutually respectful.

🌿
Sustainability

We carbon-offset every tour, employ only local communities, source food from local farms, and channel 5% of revenue into conservation and education projects across the island.

❤️
Community

We believe travel should leave a destination better than it was found. We partner with village cooperatives, women's craft collectives and grassroots conservation groups in every region we operate.
