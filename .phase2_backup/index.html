Our Custodianship & Heritage

Where Living History Breathes in Every Granite Stone

Founded in 2014 by architectural historians and island conservationists, Heritage Travels was born from a singular conviction: genuine culture cannot be rushed from a tourist coach.

Sri Lanka is home to over three millennia of recorded civilization, monumental hydraulic reservoir systems that sustained ancient megacities, and living traditions that remain vibrant today. We curate unhurried expeditions that grant you private access to sacred monastic libraries, quiet dawn walks before monument gates open, and intimate dinners in centuries-old manor courtyards.

By strictly capping our journeys at eight travelers, we safeguard the sanctity of the monuments we visit while fostering meaningful bonds with local craftsmen and historians.
