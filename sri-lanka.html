THE PEARL OF THE INDIAN OCEAN

An Island of Endless Wonder

Sri Lanka packs thousands of years of living history, misty tea-carpeted mountains, world-class wildlife, and golden palm-fringed coastlines into a compact island where every journey is unforgettable.

🍃

Central Highlands

Misty tea estates, colonial hill stations, plunging waterfalls, and the legendary Kandy-to-Ella scenic train.

🏛️

Ancient Kingdoms

Sigiriya's Sky Palace, giant stupas of Anuradhapura, cave monasteries of Dambulla, and sacred Kandy.

🐆

Wild Safaris

Highest leopard density on earth in Yala, vast elephant herds in Minneriya, and endemic tropical birds.

🌊

Golden Coastlines

Dutch colonial ramparts of Galle Fort, whale watching in Mirissa, and quiet surf bays bathed in sunset.

CURATED EXPLORATIONS

Iconic Destinations in Sri Lanka

Handcrafted routes connecting the island's most evocative landscapes.

Central Highlands
Ella & Tea Country
Watch the blue train snake across the Nine Arches Bridge, hike Little Adam's Peak, and sip single-origin Ceylon tea directly from historic hillside plantations.
Year-round · Cool Explore Ella
Cultural Triangle
Sigiriya Rock Citadel
King Kashyapa's 5th-century palace in the sky, rising 200 meters above emerald jungle canopy with ancient frescoes and water gardens.
Nov – Apr Explore Sigiriya
Southern Coast
Galle Dutch Fort
Living UNESCO World Heritage ramparts with 17th-century cobblestone lanes, boutique jewelers, colonial verandas, and ocean sunset bastions.
Dec – Apr Explore Galle
Wildlife Safari
Yala National Park
Track the elusive Sri Lankan leopard, sloth bears, and herds of wild elephants through thorn scrub, lagoons, and dramatic ocean coastline.
Feb – Jul Explore Yala
Heritage Capital
Kandy Sacred City
The hill country royal capital, home to the sacred Temple of the Tooth Relic, serene Kandy Lake, and lush Peradeniya Royal Botanic Gardens.
Year-round Explore Kandy
Tropical Coast
Mirissa & South Coast
Famed Coconut Tree Hill, serene crescent beaches, seafood dining under lanterns, and blue whale watching in the warm Indian Ocean.
Nov – Apr Explore Mirissa
